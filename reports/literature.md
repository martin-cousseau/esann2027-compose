The NeurIPS proceedings hosts (proceedings.neurips.cc, papers.nips.cc, proceedings.nips.cc, papers.neurips.cc) reset at TLS through the proxy every time I tried on 2026-11-09, and WebFetch got HTTP 503. dblp served a bot challenge, ACM DL returned 403, the Wayback Machine was unreachable, and OpenReview has no mirror record. I did not fetch either, so check them by hand. `paper/v1/main.tex` cites both keys, so they will be undefined until added.
- **NeurIPS volume and page numbers** for frantar2022obc (draft: vol. 35), dutta2024accuracy, panferov2025unified and kuzmin2023pruning could not be checked, so they are dropped. The venue comes from OpenReview, plus the camera-ready footer for OBC.
- **Harma's ICLR proceedings page and PDF** were unreachable. The Appendix F quote comes from arXiv v2 (ICLR 2025 camera-ready header), so the page number in the proceedings PDF may differ.
- **Camera-ready wording**: quotes for Kim (arXiv v1, ICLR 2026 header), Dutta and Panferov (arXiv v1) and Laborde (arXiv v1) may differ slightly from the final PDFs, which sit behind the OpenReview challenge or IEEE.
- **lm-eval version used**: not recorded (the notebooks run `pip install -q -U lm-eval`). Replace v0.4.3 with the logged version's Zenodo DOI after the re-runs.
- **Part C items 5 and 7**: the venue claims come only from arXiv comments.

## 5. Notes for the revision

- Keys in `paper/v1/main.tex` to update: `harma2025effective` → `harma2025interplay` and `guo2025obr` → `guo2026obr`. `lecun1990obd` and `hassibi1993obs` are missing. All other v1 keys are kept.
- Framing:
  - Harma's "much lower" order effect for SparseGPT was measured at 6–8 bits.
  - Kim's "marginally positive" appears as an aside in the vision section.
  - SparseGPT shows 50% + 4-bit vs 3-bit only for ≥ 2.7B.
  - Zhou's sub-1B evidence is Qwen2.5-0.5B alone.
- Budget: v1 has 18 references, and Part A adds 5 (Kim, Bouriel, Zhou, Dutta, Panferov). To reach about 16, cut in this order: OBD/OBS (OBC covers that lineage), Deep Compression, JSQ (or swap it for SLiM), Panferov (if the "multiplicative" framing goes), and any benchmark not in the main table.
