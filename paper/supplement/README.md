# Supplementary tables

Generated by `scripts/make_supplement.py` from the analysis tables that `scripts/make_tables.py` writes
(`reports/analysis/*.csv`), which also generate every number of the paper; nothing here is typed by hand.
Regenerate with `python scripts/make_tables.py && python scripts/make_supplement.py`.

- `supplement.pdf` / `.tex`: one table per model with every state (seed 0), then its per-task scores and retentions;
  at the end, the layer-level controls and the weaker-first test per model and setting, and the calibration seeds
  (mean and sd of each order, worst order of each seed).
- `complete_results.csv`: one row per model state at seed 0, including the extra states outside the paper's grid
  (8-bit, 2-bit and 60% sparsity on the small models: 30 rows per small model against the paper's 25). Columns: `order` (dense, quant, prune, joint, p_then_q,
  q_then_p), `sparsity_pct`, `bits`, `tcr` = 1 - b/16 (1 - s); `wikitext2_ppl`, `c4_ppl`; `wikitext2_kl` and `c4_kl`
  (KL divergence to the dense model; Qwen2.5-3B has none); `layer_error` (mean normalized layer error E over the
  linear layers); `I_E_pct` (layer interaction of a combination in % of E_P + E_Q, mean over layers, as in Fig. 1);
  `sr3` (semantic retention on ARC-Easy acc, PIQA acc, HellaSwag acc_norm, the paper's Sr) and `sr9` (nine tasks)
  with 95% paired-bootstrap intervals; `I_Sr3` = Sr3 of the combination minus the product of the single methods'
  Sr3, with its interval (Table 2); `I_Sr3_cn` the same with chance-corrected retention (each task score minus its
  chance level: 0.25 for ARC-Easy and HellaSwag, 0.5 for PIQA), with its interval.
- `task_scores.csv`: per state and task, the score (`score`, metric in `metric`), the dense model's score and the
  retention score / dense score, with the metric of Sr3 and Table 2 for the paper's three tasks (acc for ARC-Easy and
  PIQA, acc_norm for HellaSwag; Sr3 is the sum of these scores over the sum of the dense scores) and the metric of Sr9
  for the other six; `sr9_metric`, `sr9_score` and `sr9_dense_score` give every task with the metric of Sr9. Tasks: ARC-Easy, PIQA, HellaSwag (the paper's three), ARC-Challenge, WinoGrande,
  OpenBookQA, BoolQ, SciQ and LAMBADA (OpenAI), full splits, zero-shot.
- `seeds.csv`: WikiText-2 perplexity for calibration seeds 0, 1 and 2 with mean and sample sd (the three small
  models and Qwen2.5-1.5B).
- `layer_ablation.csv`: the ablation with one shared Hessian (every order calibrated with the dense model's Hessian;
  sums over the linear layers of every second or fourth decoder block), per model and setting: `I_E_*_pct`, the layer
  interaction of each order in % of E_P + E_Q; `gap_pct` = 100 (E_QP - E_PQ) / E_PQ and its exact split into
  `mask_pct` (mask chosen on rounded weights instead of the pruning-only mask), `order_pct` (same mask and grid, the
  second sweep started from quantized instead of pruned weights) and `grid_pct` (grid fitted before pruning instead
  of on the kept weights).
- `no_compensation.csv`: the same layers without error compensation (sweeps on the diagonal of the Hessian, errors
  with the full Hessian), per model and setting: `I_E_mask_first_pct` (mask of pruning alone, grid of quantization
  alone: the setting of the paper's decomposition), `I_E_p_then_q_pct` (grid fitted on the kept weights),
  `I_E_q_then_p_pct`; `absorption_pct` and `cross_pct`, the terms of the decomposition in % of E_P + E_Q (they sum to
  `I_E_mask_first_pct`); `gap_pct`, `mask_pct`, `grid_pct` as above (the update-order part is zero by construction);
  `zeros_of_rounding_pct`, the share of the weights that rounding alone sets to zero, and
  `pruned_weights_among_them_pct`, the share of the pruned weights that are among those zeros;
  `E_P_comp_over_nocomp` and `E_Q_comp_over_nocomp`, the single-method errors with compensation divided by those
  without.
- `weaker_first.csv`: per model and setting, the WikiText-2 perplexity of each method alone and of the three orders,
  the method a weaker-first rule puts first (`rule_recommends`: `q_then_p` when quantization alone has the lower
  perplexity), and the best and worst order by perplexity, by KL and by nine-task accuracy (empty where not measured).

All files hold calibration seed 0 except `seeds.csv`. The per-layer lines behind the two layer-level tables are in
`results/v2/E2/` and `results/v2/E9/`; all raw result lines are in `results/v2/`.
